# PHASE 10 — Exchange & Buyback

Build jewellery exchange and old-gold buyback workflows.

## Exchange

```text
Customer
 ↓
Old Jewellery
 ↓
Weight Check
 ↓
Purity Check
 ↓
Valuation
 ↓
Approval
 ↓
New Jewellery Sale
```

Create a professional valuation screen.

Display:

```text
Gross Weight
Stone Weight
Net Metal Weight
Purity
Current Gold Rate
Deduction
Exchange Value
```

Then:

```text
New Jewellery Price
- Exchange Value
------------------
Customer Payable
```

## Buyback

Support:

- Jewellery identification
- Weight
- Purity
- Valuation
- Approval
- Payment
- Inventory disposition

Every exchange/buyback operation must be linked to the customer and relevant jewellery item records.
