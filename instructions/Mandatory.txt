One important rule

Don't move to the next phase until the current phase is actually integrated and working with your backend.

For example, in Phase 4, don't just create beautiful inventory screens with dummy data. Connect them to the real APIs (pvj-backend) and verify the complete flow:

Admin
  ↓
Create Product
  ↓
Create Jewellery Item
  ↓
Assign Metal + Stone
  ↓
Assign RFID / QR
  ↓

  ↓

View Movement History
